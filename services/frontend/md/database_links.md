Access these essential resources for ID retrieval and data verification:

- **[KEGG](https://www.genome.jp/kegg/)**  
  *Pathways, genes, compounds, and metabolic networks.*

- **[BiGG Models](http://bigg.ucsd.edu/)**  
  *Genome-scale metabolic models (GSM).*

- **[SABIO-RK](https://sabiork.h-its.org/)**  
  *Biochemical reactions and kinetics data.*

- **[Rhea](https://www.rhea-db.org/)**  
  *Expert-curated resource of biochemical reactions.*
